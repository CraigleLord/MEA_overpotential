## Fellowship Mechanisms (Strongly Recommended for Korean Nationals)

### A. JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowships for Research in Japan
- **What:** Competitive fellowship placing non-Japanese PhD holders in Japanese research institutions; ~145 fellowships awarded per recruitment round
- **Eligibility:** Open to non-Japanese nationals (Korean nationals fully eligible)
- **Duration:** Up to 24 months
- **2026 status:** First round of FY2026 already awarded; watch for second round announcement
- **List of FY2026 fellows:** [jsps.go.jp FY2026 list (PDF)](https://www.jsps.go.jp/file/storage/saiyo_2026/2026_1-e.pdf)
- **Main page:** [jsps.go.jp/english/e-fellow](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-fellow/)

### B. MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships (Marie Curie) — **DEADLINE: 9 September 2026**
- **What:** EU-funded postdoctoral fellowship at a European host institution; covers salary + research costs for 12–24 months
- **Eligibility:** Open to ALL nationalities. Korean nationals can apply. Requires a European host institution to co-submit.
- **How it works:** Find a European PI willing to host you → write proposal together → submit by deadline
- **2026 call:** Open **9 April – 9 September 2026** — this is live right now
- **Action:** Contact NTNU group (item 5 above) or DTU group (item 4) as potential MSCA hosts
- **Main page:** [marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)

### C. PNNL Linus Pauling Fellowship
- See item 3 above. Researcher proposes their own project — applicant designs the research agenda.
